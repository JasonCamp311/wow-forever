Professions

Which two professions to take for your class, what Forever changed, and the cheap way to level each one.

How professions work in Forever

  • Two primary professions, plus Cooking, First Aid and Fishing for free. No new professions; the cap is still 300.
  • Your first quest in every starting zone hands out a gathering manual, so you can start Mining, Herbalism or Skinning without a trainer.
  • Ranks now need a character level too. Gathering opens earlier so you can farm while leveling.
  • Cooking matters for leveling: every cooked meal gives +5% XP from kills for 15 minutes.
  • First Aid now makes healing potions, from Peacebloom, Briarthorn and Bruiseweed.
  • Over 600 new recipes. Many leveling crafts are bind on pickup, so you only get them by having the profession.
  • No passive bonuses: Mining no longer gives Stamina, and Skinning no longer gives crit.
  • A new reagent bag slot. Tailors make the bags (6 and 12 slots).
  • Waylaid Supplies crates return. Turn them in for Merchant's Favor, which buys recipes and profession titles.
  • Every profession has camp objects that buff your group for an hour. Tier 1 comes at skill 20 from the starting-zone Camping 101 quest. Tiers 2 and 3 come from blueprints dropped by dungeon bosses.
  • Reaching 150, 225 and 300 can earn account-wide Legacy points. Sources disagree on whether gathering professions count.

Pick your pair

Level with your armor crafter and the gatherer that feeds it, then swap one at 60 if your role wants something else. Gnomes lean Engineering (20% fewer backfires) and Tauren lean Herbalism (Cultivation).

Want gold more than gear? Take two gatherers in launch week: the auction house starts empty, and Mining + Herbalism has the most buyers. Sell with Auctionator.
