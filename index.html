A field guide for World of Warcraft: Forever

Forever, turned all the way up.

The graphics menu stops short of what the engine can draw. Type these into chat and Azeroth gets thick grass to the horizon, distant hills that keep their shape, and water that reflects the trees.

GeneralConsole

Click a line to copy it, paste into chat, press Enter. Chat runs one line at a time.

Settings save to WTF/Config.wtf in your _forever_ folder. Copy that file somewhere first; if anything goes wrong, put the copy back.

I

Make it look better

Do these in order. The commands only add detail on top of the in-game preset, so the preset comes first.

  1. ! Set the graphics preset to 10

    Options → Graphics. A modern high-end card runs everything at maximum, including Secondary Lighting, Compute Effects and SSAO, the settings that draw the new fog and moonlight. Keep Render Scale at 100%.

    Reward: volumetric fog, moonlight in Ashenvale

  2. ! Paste the hidden commands

    Each one pushes a setting past where its slider stops.

    CommandWhat changes
    groundEffectDensity 256Grass and flowers. The menu maximum is 48; 256 turns the Barrens and Elwynn into real fields. Drop to 128 if frame rate suffers.
    groundEffectDist 500Grass is drawn out to 500 yards instead of about 200.
    groundEffectFade 370Grass fades in gently at the edge instead of popping.
    lodObjectFadeScale 200Carts, wells, fences and signs stay visible from further away.
    lodObjectCullSize 8Small distant props stop being hidden.
    doodadLodScale 200Trees and rocks keep full detail further out.
    terrainLodDist 1000Distant hills keep their shape and textures.
    reflectionMode 3Water reflects trees, characters and mounts, not just the sky.
    weatherDensity 3Heaviest rain and snow. Storms last about 20 minutes.
    ResampleAlwaysSharpen 1Sharpening at 100% render scale. Forever looks soft without it.
    cameraDistanceMaxZoomFactor 4Lets the camera pull back about twice as far.

    Reward: grass to the horizon

  3. ! Tune sharpness and colour

    After the sharpen command, set Resample Sharpness (bottom of Graphics) to about 0.6. Higher looks grainy.

    The new fog and lighting can take far more contrast than old WoW could. Start at:

    Contrast
    75–80
    Brightness
    48
    Gamma
    1.1

    Raise gamma, not brightness, if caves are too dark to see the floor.

  4. ! Smooth out frame pacing

    Turn vsync off, cap the foreground frame rate a few frames under your monitor's refresh rate (160 on a 165 Hz panel), and turn on low-latency mode. A capped game runs cooler and more evenly than one hitting 400 fps in Goldshire.

  5. ? Optional: RTX HDR

    On an HDR monitor with an NVIDIA RTX card, RTX HDR in the NVIDIA app works at the driver level without touching the game. Install the NVIDIA driver released for Forever on 4 November first.

II

What to skip

Cursed items. They look tempting and cost more than they give.

ReShade

Modern WoW has blocked ReShade's dxgi.dll since 2025, players report bans, and Forever runs on the same client. The commands above get most of the same look safely.

NVIDIA filters workaround

Alt+F3 filters stopped recognising Forever at launch. The anselintegrationtestapp.exe trick going around the forums breaks chat channels. Wait for a driver fix.

Ray Traced Shadows

Greyed out for everyone. Blizzard pulled it from the beta on 1 October and the client reports no ray tracing on any card. Forcing the setting does nothing.

WeakAuras

No official Forever version. Modern WoW's combat rules stop addons from doing that kind of maths in combat. Use the built-in Cooldown Manager instead.

Built-in tools worth turning on

Forever ships features that used to need addons. Most are off by default. Tick them off as you go; this page remembers your progress in this browser and fills your XP bar.

IV

Addons

Forever only loads addon files built for it (interface 16001). Every addon here has a Forever release on CurseForge. Hover a card to inspect it. The leveling guide tells you when to open each one.

Questie

Binds when installed · Questing

Quest givers, objectives and turn-ins on the map, including quests you haven't picked up.

Use: /questie

"Some brand-new Forever quests are still being added."

Baganator

Binds when installed · Bags

All bags in one window with search and categories, plus what your alts carry. Requires Syndicator.

Use: /bgr

Leatrix Plus

Binds when installed · Comfort

Dozens of small fixes: sell junk, auto-repair, faster loot, bigger zoom. Every option starts off.

Use: /ltp

"Take the file ending in -forever, not the Classic one."

DungeonJournal

Binds when installed · Dungeons

Maps, quests, bosses and loot for every dungeon in one window. RPG mode hides pages until you clear the dungeon.

Use: /dj

Deadly Boss Mods

Binds when installed · Combat

Boss timers and warnings, with the Vanilla raid and Forever dungeon modules. Raids open 9 December.

Use: /dbm

Auctionator

Binds when installed · Gold

Prices in tooltips, shopping lists and quick posting at the Auction House.

Use: open the Auction House

ForeverThreat

Binds when installed · Combat

Omen-style threat meter made for Forever, with a pull warning. Forever has no built-in threat display.

Use: /ft

ForeverBiS

Binds when installed · Gear

Best-in-slot lists for all 28 specs that track what you own, plus loot and quests for every dungeon and the dungeon maps the client lacks.

Use: /bis

Pawn

Binds when installed · Gear

Green upgrade arrows on tooltips, using a stat scale for your current spec. The fastest way to know whether a quest reward is better.

Use: /pawn

AtlasLoot Forever

Binds when installed · Gear

Loot tables by boss with BiS tooltips. Plan which dungeon drops your next upgrade.

Use: /atlasloot

"The one by Sliccer. The old AtlasLootClassic has no Forever file."

RestedXP Guide

Binds when installed · Leveling

Step-by-step route with an arrow. The free route runs to about 20 (Alliance) or 22 (Horde), which pairs with the leveling guide here.

Use: /rxp

Leatrix Maps

Binds when installed · Maps

Reveals unexplored map areas, shows zone levels and coordinates.

Use: /ltm

TomTom

Binds when installed · Maps

A waypoint arrow. Point it at any coordinate.

Use: /way 45 60

GatherMate2

Binds when installed · Professions

Remembers every herb, ore and fishing node you find and pins it on the map.

Use: /gathermate

Install or update them all

Pick WoW Forever as the game version in the CurseForge app and install each one, or run the script in this repo with the game closed. It fetches each addon's newest Forever file and copies only the folders that load in Forever.

python -I tools/install_addons.py "C:/Program Files (x86)/World of Warcraft/_forever_/Interface/AddOns"

Use one addon manager only. Two apps updating the same folder overwrite each other.

Worth a look later

  • Platynator or Plater for nameplates you can restyle.
  • EllesmereUI or ElvUI to replace the whole interface. Try Edit Mode first.
  • Guidelime for free leveling routes once RestedXP's free route ends at 20.

No Forever version yet

  • WeakAuras (community forks exist, expect breakage)
  • Postal
  • OmniCC
  • Recount
