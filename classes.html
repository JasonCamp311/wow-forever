How Forever talents work

  • Three trees, 51 points, first point at 10. The account-wide Legacy perk Talented can move that down to level 5 on alts.
  • New: every tree has a fourth one-point "gold" talent in row 4, reached with your 16th point in the tree, at level 25 if you go straight down. It sits alongside the classic 11, 21 and 31-point talents.
  • Talents that only boosted a buff are now baseline: Improved Battle Shout, Divine Spirit, Blessing of Kings, Improved Mark of the Wild.
  • Respec at your class trainer in a capital. Dual specialization opens at 40.
  • Build guides dated before 4 November are stale: Lava Burst, Binding Heal, Piercing Ice, Berserk and Tiger's Fury are gone.
